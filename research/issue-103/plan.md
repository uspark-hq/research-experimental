# Research Plan for Issue #103

## Intent Analysis
**Keywords/Concepts Identified:** Spain (西班牙), Climate Environment (气候环境), Road Infrastructure (路面环境), Consumer Vehicle Demand, Temperature, Humidity, Precipitation, Dust, Road Network Scale, Road Quality, Special Road Conditions

**Inferred Research Intent:** To validate and analyze how Spain's climate conditions and road infrastructure influence consumer preferences and demand patterns for passenger vehicles in the Spanish market.

**Key Questions to Answer:**
1. Does climate and road infrastructure truly affect consumer vehicle demand in Spain?
2. What are the specific climate characteristics of Spain that impact vehicle usage?
3. What are the road infrastructure characteristics in Spain that influence vehicle preferences?
4. How do these environmental factors translate into specific consumer vehicle preferences?
5. What vehicle features or types are most demanded due to these conditions?

## Research Tasks
- [ ] Task 1: Validate the hypothesis - analyze correlation between environmental factors and vehicle demand in Spain
- [ ] Task 2: Deep analysis of Spain's climate environment (temperature variations, humidity levels, precipitation patterns, dust/sand conditions, extreme weather events)
- [ ] Task 3: Comprehensive analysis of Spain's road infrastructure (network size, road quality, special conditions, terrain challenges)
- [ ] Task 4: Analyze Spanish consumer vehicle preferences and market trends influenced by environmental factors
- [ ] Task 5: Synthesize findings and create comprehensive report in Simplified Chinese

## Expected Outcomes
- Clear validation of whether climate and road conditions impact vehicle demand in Spain
- Detailed understanding of Spain's climate characteristics affecting vehicle usage
- Comprehensive analysis of Spain's road infrastructure and its impact on vehicle preferences
- Specific consumer preferences and market trends driven by environmental factors
- Strategic insights for automotive manufacturers targeting the Spanish market
