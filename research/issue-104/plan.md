# Research Plan for Issue #104

## Intent Analysis
**Keywords/Concepts Identified:** 法国 (France), 气候环境 (climate environment), 路面环境 (road infrastructure), 乘用车市场 (passenger car market), consumer preferences

**Inferred Research Intent:** The user wants to understand how France's climate conditions and road infrastructure influence consumer demand and preferences for passenger vehicles, testing the hypothesis that these environmental factors significantly impact vehicle purchase decisions.

**Key Questions to Answer:**
- What are France's specific climate characteristics that affect vehicle operation?
- How does France's road infrastructure quality and network influence vehicle requirements?
- Do these factors measurably impact French consumer vehicle preferences?
- What vehicle features are prioritized due to these environmental conditions?

## Research Tasks
- [ ] Task 1: Deep analysis of France's climate environment (temperature, humidity, precipitation, dust, seasonal variations)
- [ ] Task 2: Comprehensive study of France's road infrastructure (network scale, quality, special conditions, terrain)
- [ ] Task 3: Analysis of French consumer vehicle preferences and market trends
- [ ] Task 4: Correlation between environmental factors and vehicle demand patterns
- [ ] Task 5: Comparison with other European markets to validate hypothesis

## Expected Outcomes
- Clear validation of whether climate and road conditions influence French vehicle demand
- Detailed data on France's climate patterns affecting vehicle operation
- Comprehensive assessment of French road infrastructure characteristics
- Evidence-based insights on consumer preferences shaped by environmental factors
- Actionable recommendations for automotive market strategies in France
