# Task 3: Google Gemini Evolution (2023-2024)

## Version Release Timeline

### 2023 - From Bard to Gemini
- **February 2023**: [Google CEO announced Bard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_(chatbot)) powered by LaMDA
- **March 21, 2023**: [Bard opened for early access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_(chatbot)) in US and UK
- **May 10, 2023**: [Gemini announced at Google I/O](https://timelines.issarice.com/wiki/Timeline_of_Google_Gemini)
- **December 6, 2023**: [Gemini 1.0 announced](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_(language_model)) (Ultra, Pro, Flash, Nano versions)
- **December 13, 2023**: Gemini Pro available via API

### 2024 - Unification and Expansion
- **January 2024**: [Partnership with Samsung](https://timelines.issarice.com/wiki/Timeline_of_Google_Gemini) for Galaxy S24 integration
- **February 8, 2024**: [Bard renamed to Gemini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_(chatbot)), mobile app launched
- **February 2024**: [Gemini 1.5 launched](https://www.britannica.com/technology/Google-Gemini) in limited capacity
- **May 14, 2024**: [Gemini 1.5 Flash announced](https://timelines.issarice.com/wiki/Timeline_of_Google_Gemini) at I/O 2024
- **September 24, 2024**: [Gemini-1.5-Pro-002 and Flash-002 released](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/learn/model-versions)
- **December 11, 2024**: [Gemini 2.0 Flash Experimental announced](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/changelog)

## Pricing Evolution

### Major Price Reduction (October 2024)
According to [Google's announcement](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/updated-gemini-models-reduced-15-pro-pricing-increased-rate-limits-and-more/):
- **64% reduction** on input tokens
- **52% reduction** on output tokens
- **64% reduction** on incremental cached tokens
- Effective October 1, 2024 for prompts under 128K tokens

### Current Pricing Structure

**Free Tier** ([per documentation](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing)):
- Gemini 1.5 Flash: 15 requests/min, 1M tokens/min, 1,500 requests/day
- Gemini 1.5 Pro: 2 requests/min, 32K tokens/min, 50 requests/day
- Gemini 1.0 Pro: 15 requests/min, 1M tokens/min, 1,500 requests/day

**Paid Features**:
- [Batch API offers 50% discount](https://www.cloudeagle.ai/blogs/blogs-google-gemini-pricing-guide) on interactive requests
- [2x higher rate limits on Flash, 3x on Pro](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/updated-gemini-models-reduced-15-pro-pricing-increased-rate-limits-and-more/)

### Subscription Tiers
**Gemini Advanced** ([via Google One AI Premium](https://www.uctoday.com/collaboration/gemini-pricing-everything-youll-pay-for-google-gemini/)):
- $19.99/month
- Two-month free trial
- Includes 2TB cloud storage

**Enterprise Options** ([Google Cloud pricing](https://cloud.google.com/products/gemini/pricing)):
- Code Assist: $19/user/month (standard), $22.80/user/month (premium)
- Free for one user per billing account until November 8, 2024

## Model Family Structure

### Core Models
Per [Google's model documentation](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/learn/model-versions):
- **Nano**: On-device tasks, Android features
- **Flash**: Fast, efficient for high-volume tasks
- **Pro**: Mid-tier balanced model
- **Ultra**: Most powerful, available with Advanced subscription

### Key Capabilities Evolution
- **Multimodal**: Text, images, audio, video, code support
- **Context Windows**: Competitive with other providers
- **Speed**: 1.5 Flash optimized for rapid responses

## Strategic Observations

1. **Brand Consolidation**: Unified Bard and Duet AI under Gemini brand
2. **Aggressive Pricing**: 50-64% price cuts in 2024 to compete
3. **Free Tier Generosity**: Substantial free usage limits
4. **Mobile First**: Early Android app launch and device integration
5. **Enterprise Focus**: Special pricing for cloud customers
6. **Rapid Iteration**: Major updates every 3-4 months in 2024

## Data Usage Policy
According to [pricing documentation](https://www.cloudeagle.ai/blogs/blogs-google-gemini-pricing-guide):
- Free tier: Experiments may train future models
- Paid tier: Prompts/responses not used to improve products
