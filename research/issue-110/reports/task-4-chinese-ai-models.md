# Task 4: Chinese AI Models Evolution (2023-2025)

## ByteDance Doubao (豆包)

### Version History
**2023 Releases**:
- **August 2023**: [First LLM "Skylark" (云雀) released](https://aiproem.substack.com/p/bytedances-ai-legacy-and-strategy)
- **August 2023**: [Doubao chatbot debuted](https://recodechinaai.substack.com/p/tiktoks-parent-bytedance-quietly), initially underwhelming with 1.3M DAU by November

**2024 Updates**:
- **May 2024**: [Skylark rebranded to Doubao](https://technode.com/2024/05/16/bytedance-surprises-ai-rivals-with-ultra-low-cost-doubao-model/)
- **June 11, 2024**: [Doubao 1.6 released](https://news.aibase.com/news/18817) at Force Original Power Conference
- **August 2024**: Major upgrade to visual and speech capabilities

**2025 Latest**:
- **January 22, 2025**: [Doubao-1.5-pro released](https://medium.com/towards-agi/bytedance-just-dropped-doubao-1-5-pro-the-ai-model-thats-quietly-outsmarting-everyone-b9ebc523558d), outperforming OpenAI's o1 on AIME benchmark

### Pricing Revolution
According to [ByteDance's announcement](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2024-05-16/bytedance-enters-ai-arena-with-doubao-offering-ultra-low-cost-and-versatile-applications-102196732.html):
- **Initial (May 2024)**: Processing 1.25 million tokens for only $0.14
- **99.8% cheaper than GPT-4** at launch
- **Current pricing**:
  - Doubao Pro: RMB 0.8 (~$0.11) per million tokens
  - Doubao-1.5-pro-32k: ¥0.8 ($0.11) per million input tokens, ¥2 output
  - Doubao-1.5-lite-32k: ¥0.3 per million input tokens

### Model Family
[Nine models total](https://connectcx.ai/bytedance-unveils-doubao-ai-models-entering-the-competitive-language-model-arena/) including:
- Doubao Pro (128K tokens capacity)
- Doubao Lite (entry-level)
- Specialized models for audio and text-to-image

## DeepSeek

### Version Timeline
**2023**:
- **November 2023**: [DeepSeek Coder released](https://www.oneclickitsolution.com/centerofexcellence/aiml/deepseek-ai-versions-breakdown-detailed-guide-to-every-versions) - first open source model
- **December 2023**: [DeepSeek LLM released](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepSeek) with 67B parameters

**2024**:
- **May 2024**: [DeepSeek-V2 released](https://www.helicone.ai/blog/deepseek-v3)
- **July 2024**: DeepSeek-Coder-V2 (236B parameters, 128K context)
- **November 2024**: R1-Lite-Preview released
- **December 2024**: [DeepSeek-V3 released](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/news/news1226) with 671B parameters

**2025**:
- **January 2025**: [DeepSeek-R1 released](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/what-is-deepseek-everything-you-need-to-know/) under MIT License

### Pricing & Training Costs
Per [DeepSeek documentation](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing):
- **V3 Pricing**: $0.27/million input tokens, $1.10/million output tokens
- **20-50x cheaper than OpenAI's o1** for R1 model
- [Training cost only $5.6 million](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/deepseek-v3-and-the-actual-cost-of) vs $100M for GPT-4

### Performance Highlights
- [V3 is 3x faster than V2](https://www.helicone.ai/blog/deepseek-v3) (60 tokens/second)
- Matches GPT-4 and Claude 3.5 Sonnet performance
- Open-source under permissive licenses

## Market Dynamics

### Pricing War
1. **ByteDance Doubao**: Leading with 99.8% discount vs GPT-4
2. **DeepSeek**: Achieving similar performance at 1/20th to 1/50th the cost
3. **Price cuts up to 70%** in enterprise trials

### Development Efficiency
- Chinese models trained at fraction of Western costs
- DeepSeek V3: $5.6M training cost vs GPT-4's $100M
- Rapid iteration cycles: major updates every 2-3 months

### Strategic Observations
1. **Ultra-aggressive pricing** as primary market entry strategy
2. **Open-source approach** (DeepSeek, Qwen) vs closed Western models
3. **Government approval requirement** for public release in China
4. **Focus on efficiency**: Lower training costs, faster inference
5. **Rapid user acquisition**: Baidu claiming 300M users in ~1 year
