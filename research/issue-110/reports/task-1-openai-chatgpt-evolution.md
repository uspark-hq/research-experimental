# Task 1: OpenAI/ChatGPT Evolution (2022-2024)

## Version Release Timeline

### 2022 - The Launch
- **November 30, 2022**: [OpenAI launched ChatGPT](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/history-of-chatgpt-timeline/488370/) using GPT-3.5 as part of a free research preview
- **December 2022**: OpenAI released text-embedding-ada-002

### 2023 - Rapid Expansion
- **February 1, 2023**: [OpenAI announced ChatGPT Plus](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-plus/), a premium subscription at $20/month
- **March 1, 2023**: OpenAI introduced the ChatGPT API for developers
- **March 14, 2023**: [OpenAI released GPT-4](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/history-of-chatgpt-timeline/488370/) promising better reliability, creativity, and problem-solving
- **April 23, 2023**: ChatGPT plugins and browsing capabilities launched in ALPHA
- **May 15, 2023**: ChatGPT iOS app launched
- **November 6, 2023**: Custom GPTs announced, enabling users to build their own GPT versions
- **November 2023**: GPT-4 Turbo launched with 128,000 token context window

### 2024 - Performance & Cost Revolution
- **January 10, 2024**: GPT Store launched and ChatGPT Team introduced
- **April 9, 2024**: Plugins phased out in favor of custom GPTs
- **May 13, 2024**: [GPT-4o ("omni") introduced](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/history-of-chatgpt-timeline/488370/) - multimodal model processing text, images, and audio
- **July 18, 2024**: GPT-4o mini released, replacing GPT-3.5 Turbo
- **July 25, 2024**: SearchGPT prototype launched
- **October 2024**: ChatGPT Search introduced
- **December 2024**: Phone calling feature launched

## Pricing Evolution

### API Pricing Trajectory
- **June 2023**: [25% cost reduction for GPT-3.5-turbo](https://tech.co/news/chatgpt-openai-update-price-reduction) to $0.0015/1k input tokens
- **November 2023**: [GPT-4 Turbo priced 3x cheaper than GPT-4](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/11/06/openai-announces-more-powerful-gpt-4-turbo-and-cuts-prices.html) at $0.01 input/$0.03 output
- **January 2024**: [Further GPT-3.5 Turbo price drop](https://medium.com/@pereiradomnic44/title-openai-announces-price-drop-for-gpt-3-5-5e87690c6fe5)
- **2024 Current**: GPT-3.5-turbo-0125 at $0.0005/1k input and $0.0015/1k output
- **2025**: [80% price drop for o3 model](https://venturebeat.com/ai/openai-announces-80-price-drop-for-o3-its-most-powerful-reasoning-model) to $2/million input tokens

### Subscription Pricing
- **ChatGPT Plus**: Maintained at $20/month since February 2023 launch
- **Free Tier**: Consistently available with limited access
- **New Tiers (2024)**: Plus, Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans introduced

## Key Observations

1. **Rapid Release Cycle**: Major updates every 2-3 months throughout 2023-2024
2. **Consistent Price Reductions**: API costs dropped by over 90% from initial pricing
3. **Feature Evolution**: From text-only to multimodal capabilities
4. **Subscription Stability**: ChatGPT Plus price unchanged despite massive capability improvements
5. **Market Strategy**: Aggressive pricing cuts to maintain leadership against competitors
