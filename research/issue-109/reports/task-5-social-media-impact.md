# Task 5: Social Media Strategy and Impact on Brand/Revenue

Hard Rock International has successfully leveraged social media platforms including TikTok and Instagram to enhance brand engagement, drive revenue, and adapt to digital-first consumer behavior, turning potential disruption into competitive advantage through strategic multi-platform campaigns and AI-powered optimization.
