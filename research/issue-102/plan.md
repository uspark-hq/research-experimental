# Research Plan for Issue #102

## Intent Analysis
**Keywords/Concepts Identified:**
- Germany (德国) climate environment (气候环境)
- Road infrastructure (路面环境)
- Consumer vehicle preferences in relation to environmental factors
- Impact of climate (temperature, humidity, precipitation, dust) on driving
- Impact of road conditions (network scale, quality, special conditions) on driving

**Inferred Research Intent:**
The user wants to understand how Germany's specific climate conditions and road infrastructure characteristics influence consumer preferences and demand for passenger vehicles, likely to inform market strategy or product development decisions.

**Key Questions to Answer:**
1. Does climate and road infrastructure significantly affect vehicle consumer preferences in Germany?
2. What are the specific climate characteristics of Germany that impact vehicle usage?
3. What are the road infrastructure characteristics that influence vehicle choice?
4. How do German consumers adapt their vehicle preferences to local conditions?
5. What vehicle features are prioritized due to climate and road conditions?

## Research Tasks
- [ ] Task 1: Comprehensive analysis of Germany's climate conditions affecting vehicle usage
- [ ] Task 2: Detailed assessment of Germany's road infrastructure and special conditions
- [ ] Task 3: Validate hypothesis - relationship between environment and consumer vehicle preferences
- [ ] Task 4: Analysis of vehicle features/types preferred due to climate factors
- [ ] Task 5: Analysis of vehicle features/types preferred due to road conditions
- [ ] Task 6: Comparative data on vehicle sales by type and regional variations

## Expected Outcomes
- Clear validation of whether climate/road conditions influence German vehicle consumer preferences
- Detailed climate profile of Germany with automotive impact analysis
- Comprehensive road infrastructure assessment with driving implications
- Specific consumer preference patterns linked to environmental factors
- Strategic insights for automotive market positioning in Germany
