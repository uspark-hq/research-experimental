# Research Plan for Issue #107

## Intent Analysis
**Keywords/Concepts Identified:** 澳大利亚(Australia), 气候环境(climate environment), 路面环境(road infrastructure), 乘用车市场(passenger car market), consumer demand
**Inferred Research Intent:** To analyze how Australia's unique climate conditions and road infrastructure characteristics influence passenger vehicle consumer preferences and market demand
**Key Questions to Answer:**
- Does Australia's climate significantly affect vehicle purchasing decisions?
- What specific climate factors (temperature extremes, UV exposure, rainfall patterns, dust) impact vehicle requirements?
- How does Australia's vast road network and varied terrain influence vehicle choices?
- What vehicle features are prioritized due to these environmental factors?

## Research Tasks
- [ ] Task 1: Validate hypothesis - determine if climate/road conditions affect Australian vehicle demand
- [ ] Task 2: Deep analysis of Australian climate environment (temperature, humidity, precipitation, dust storms, UV radiation)
- [ ] Task 3: Comprehensive study of Australian road infrastructure (network scale, quality variations, remote area access, special conditions)
- [ ] Task 4: Consumer preference analysis linking environmental factors to vehicle choices
- [ ] Task 5: Market data synthesis showing popular vehicle types and features

## Expected Outcomes
- Clear validation of whether environmental factors influence vehicle purchasing
- Detailed climate profile relevant to vehicle operation
- Comprehensive road infrastructure assessment
- Evidence-based insights on consumer preferences
- Actionable recommendations for automotive market strategies
