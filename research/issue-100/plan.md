# Research Plan for Issue #100

## Intent Analysis
**Keywords/Concepts Identified:** 德国 (Germany), 气候环境 (climate environment), 路面环境 (road environment), 乘用车驾驶 (passenger car driving)
**Inferred Research Intent:** Analyze how Germany's climate and road conditions impact automotive consumer demands and preferences for passenger vehicles
**Key Questions to Answer:**
- How do Germany's climate patterns (temperature, humidity, precipitation, dust/particulates) affect vehicle performance requirements?
- What are the characteristics of Germany's road network (scale, quality, special conditions) that influence vehicle preferences?
- Is there a verifiable correlation between these environmental factors and consumer automotive demands?
- What specific vehicle features and capabilities are prioritized by German consumers due to these conditions?

## Research Tasks
- [ ] Task 1: Deep dive into Germany's road infrastructure (Autobahn network, urban roads, rural roads, construction materials, maintenance standards)
- [ ] Task 2: Comprehensive analysis of Germany's climate patterns (seasonal variations, regional differences, extreme weather events)
- [ ] Task 3: Investigate correlation between environmental factors and German automotive market preferences
- [ ] Task 4: Research in both English and German languages for comprehensive coverage
- [ ] Task 5: Compile data tables with concrete statistics and verified sources

## Expected Outcomes
- Detailed understanding of Germany's unique road and climate conditions
- Clear evidence of how these factors influence vehicle design and consumer preferences
- Data-backed insights on specific automotive features demanded by German consumers
- Executive summaries on both road and climate environments
- Comprehensive tables with statistics and original source links
