# Research Plan for Issue #101

## Intent Analysis
**Keywords/Concepts Identified:** 德国 (Germany), 法国 (France), 意大利 (Italy), 西班牙 (Spain), 波兰 (Poland), 汽车税费 (Automotive Taxes), EU Tax, 乘用车 (Passenger Cars), 购前与购时涉税全流程 (Pre-purchase and Purchase Tax Process), CIF→OTR (Cost Insurance Freight to On-The-Road Price)

**Inferred Research Intent:** The user requires comprehensive research on automotive taxation systems in five EU countries (Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Poland) for passenger vehicles, covering the complete tax journey from import to registration, with a focus on calculating the total tax burden from CIF to OTR prices. Special attention needed for EV/hybrid vehicle policies.

**Key Questions to Answer:**
- What are the import duties, VAT, and registration taxes for passenger vehicles in each country?
- How do these taxes differ for ICE vs. EV/PHEV vehicles?
- What is the complete calculation methodology from CIF to OTR price?
- What incentives exist for electric and hybrid vehicles?
- What are the upcoming regulatory changes in automotive taxation?
