# Research Plan for Issue #106

## Intent Analysis
**Keywords/Concepts Identified:** 波兰 (Poland), 气候环境 (climate environment), 路面环境 (road infrastructure), passenger car market, consumer demand
**Inferred Research Intent:** To understand how Poland's climate conditions and road infrastructure influence consumer preferences and demand for passenger vehicles, validating whether these environmental factors significantly impact automotive market dynamics
**Key Questions to Answer:**
- What are Poland's specific climate characteristics that affect driving conditions?
- How does Poland's road infrastructure quality and scale impact vehicle requirements?
- Can we validate that climate and road conditions significantly influence vehicle purchasing decisions in Poland?
- What specific vehicle features are prioritized by Polish consumers due to these environmental factors?

## Research Tasks
- [ ] Task 1: Comprehensive analysis of Poland's climate conditions (temperature ranges, precipitation patterns, seasonal variations, extreme weather events)
- [ ] Task 2: Detailed assessment of Poland's road infrastructure (network size, quality metrics, road types, maintenance challenges)
- [ ] Task 3: Analysis of Polish automotive market and consumer preferences influenced by environmental factors
- [ ] Task 4: Validation of hypothesis - correlation between environmental conditions and vehicle demand patterns
- [ ] Task 5: Comparative analysis with other European markets to highlight Poland's unique characteristics

## Expected Outcomes
- Comprehensive understanding of Poland's climate challenges for vehicle operation
- Detailed mapping of road infrastructure quality and its impact on vehicle requirements
- Evidence-based validation of environmental influence on consumer vehicle preferences
- Actionable insights for automotive companies targeting the Polish market
- Strategic recommendations based on climate and infrastructure considerations
